Groww Brokerage Formula

₹20 or 0.1% of trade value (whichever is lower) per executed order, subject to a minimum floor of ₹5 per executed order.

DP Charges (Delivery Only)

₹20 flat (+ 18% GST = ₹23.60) debited per scrip on sell side from demat. Intraday incurs ₹0 DP charges.

Securities Transaction Tax (STT)
  • Intraday: 0.025% on sell value only.
  • Delivery: 0.1% on buy value + 0.1% on sell value.
Other Regulatory Levies
  • Exchange Txn: NSE ~0.00297% on turnover.
  • SEBI Turnover: ₹10 per crore (0.0001%).
  • Stamp Duty: 0.003% (Intraday buy) vs 0.015% (Delivery buy).
  • GST: 18% on (Brokerage + Txn/SEBI + DP charges).
